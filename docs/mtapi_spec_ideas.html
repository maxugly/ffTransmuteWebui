mtapi spec ideas — new and categorized

Glitch & video effects — add these
new idea
pixel sort
Sort pixels by brightness/hue along scanlines or columns. Pure numpy, no AI, screams on Iris Xe. Classic glitch art staple.
overlap with ffglitch-spec — maybe merge
new idea
datamosh — echo / feedback
Accumulate and alpha-blend prior frames back in. Creates that melting VHS-loop aesthetic without any AI.
new idea
chromatic aberration
Offset R/G/B channels spatially. FFmpeg filtergraph or numpy. CPU-only, trivial, looks devastating on portraits.
new idea
scanline / CRT shader
Horizontal scanline overlay, optional barrel distortion and vignette. Pure image compositing. Low effort, high vibe.
new idea
RGB halftone
CMYK-style dot grid per channel. Looks like offset-printed misregistration. FFmpeg geq or PIL.
new idea
datamosh — slice jitter
Cut image/video into horizontal or vertical slices and offset them randomly. Zero AI, pure ffmpeg crop/overlay math.
Audio-visual / sync
new idea
beat-sync stutter
Detect onsets via librosa (CPU), cut video to repeat or freeze on transients. Looks edited by someone with taste.
librosa runs fine on this hardware
new idea
waveform burn-in
Render audio waveform or spectrum onto the video frame. FFmpeg's showwaves + overlay. Zero deps.
new idea
loudness-driven zoom
Sample RMS per frame via ffmpeg, map to scale/zoom curve, re-render. Looks reactive without needing a DJ.

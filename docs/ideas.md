1. Practical Utility FFmpeg OpsPalette-Optimized GIF / WebP Export: Standard FFmpeg GIF output looks rough without two-pass palette generation. A dedicated preset using palettegen and paletteuse filters makes crisp, tiny loopable GIFs/WebPs.Audio Loudness Normalization (loudnorm): One-click EBU R128 audio normalization so mixed sources match perceived loudness across stitched clips.Audio Swapping / Muting / Silence Generation: Quick toggles to strip audio entirely, attach a fresh MP3/WAV, or pad missing audio streams with silent AAC tracks before sequence stitching.Time-Lapse / Frame Sampling: Decimate frames (fps=1) or drop every $N$th frame to create hyperlapse or stop-motion effects without re-encoding the whole stream at high rates.LUT (Look-Up Table) Application: A drop zone for .cube / .3dl files to apply quick cinematic or surreal color profiles via lut3d.2. Weird, Niche & Glitch Video FXSlit-Scan / Time Displacement: Slicing frames so that the vertical or horizontal axis represents time instead of space (either via FFmpeg tblend/freezedetect combos or a short Python pixel loop).Video Echo / Temporal Feedback (lagfun): Feeding previous frames back into current frames with decay. FFmpeg's lagfun or tblend=all_mode=average creates eerie, liquid motion trails behind moving objects.Codec Motion Vector Overlay (codecview): Draw the raw motion vectors calculated by H.264/MPEG-4 directly over the video as arrows/grids. It looks like high-tech diagnostic HUD data and pairs visually with datamoshing.Intentional Stream Corruption (Bit-Flipping): Direct byte/hex manipulation on raw media streams (bypassing FFmpeg filters and intentionally flipping random bytes in keyframe intervals for hardware-level glitch artifacts).Audio-Reactive Waveforms (showwaves / avectorscope): Overlaying live audio oscilloscopes, Lissajous figures, or frequency spectrums over the video stream automatically.Pixel Sorting: Sorting pixel rows or columns based on threshold values (luminance, hue, or saturation) to create falling/melting digital landscapes.3. Lightweight AI / Neural AdditionsDepth Map Extraction (MiDaS / ZoeDepth): Inferring a grayscale depth map per frame. You can feed depth maps into FFmpeg or shaders to drive faux-3D parallax, focus pulls, or displacement maps.Video Frame Interpolation (RIFE / FLAVR): Generating smooth slow-motion frames locally without cloud dependencies.Optical Flow Extraction (RAFT / Farneback): Generating RGB-encoded optical flow maps (where color indicates direction and intensity indicates speed). This can be used as a mask or target for your Style Transfer and Datamosh nodes.

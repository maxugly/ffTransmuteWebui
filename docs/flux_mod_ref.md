# Black Forest Labs FLUX 3 Video Model Reference

### FLUX 3 Video
- released: 2026-07 (announced July 23, 2026; Video + Action gated Early Access immediately; image side "coming weeks", open-weight Dev later 2026; public playground free trial until Aug 17)
- category: Unified multimodal frontier (Self-Flow: image, video and audio learned simultaneously so each constrains the others; not a bolt-on video head; FLUX-mimic robotics offshoot)
- good-at: T2V + multi-frame I2V + continuation with synchronized audio from the same weights (generate_audio produces dialogue, SFX, music matched to visuals); 20-second single generations with native audio; human faces / skin texture sharper than FLUX.2 paid tools with natural micro-motion; multilingual dialogue with legit-good lip-sync ("sounds like she's in the room"); "weird believable" retro TV footage, awkward interviews, fake docs; typography / product consistency locked better than Veo; chainable into minutes via agentic chaining with reference images; 720p Draft mode as cheap coherence lab ($0.06/s draft vs $0.29/s full; other routes 6 credits/s vs 17/s full ~1:3; another breakdown $0.17/s draft, $0.43/s normal).
- do-not: Go 15-20s or 1080p first try (burns money for drift); push single generations past ~12s without keyframes (compositional drift); expect fast combat, crowds, or hands to hold (fast combat still breaks; drift past 12-15s); feed external audio (does NOT take audio input — reference images can be opening frames, audio is output-only); trust FLF transitions as physics-locked (transition test produced a blurry failed cut where Seedance 2.0 delivered clean); treat BFL's 93% vs Luma / 77% vs Runway as shipping numbers (labeled "preliminary evaluation of an early FLUX 3 candidate"; vs Seedance 2.0 and Gemini Omni Flash it is 52% — a coin flip); expect to self-host (no open weights yet, gated access).
- personality: Super-realistic close-up talker; believable micro-motion and skin over explosive physics. BFL's own benchmark was 10-second 720p T2V clips with audio; API wrappers default 4-10s.
- tips:
  1. Iterate in 720p Draft 5-10s single-subject slow/medium motion, then enhance one keeper (drafts only save money when you iterate).
  2. Lock 12s+ compositions with keyframes: flux-3-flf-draft (start_image_url + end_image_url), flux-3-keyframes-draft, or images_list T2V+I2V.
  3. Enforce prompt constraints to reduce unwanted motion, visual inconsistencies, and compositional drift; street take is 5-8s for testing, 10s for delivery, 20s only for a full ad beat.
- vs: Gets smoked by Seedance 2.5 on motion ("seedance competitor, maybe seedance 2, fails so much vs 2.5"; carousel: Seedance outperformed across dialogue, product commercials, transitions; monster clip side-by-side darker, softer, less detailed); more expensive, restrictive censorship, less organic human physique rendering, limited reference-to-video support. Beats Omni Flash on believability (Omni lip-sync great but plasticky and AI-sounding). Native training res never published (early evals 720p); API catalog flux_3_video is 5-20s 720p/1080p, the only entry with native 2:1 ratio; wrappers expose 480p/720p/1080p; max 1080p, no 4K.
- sources: BFL announcement (July 23, 2026); API catalog (flux_3_video); wrapper docs; r/aivideo + Threads creator breakdowns

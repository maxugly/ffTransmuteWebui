# TTS (Text-to-Speech) Models Supertable

Merged from the supplied research and 2026 TTS breakdown. Claims, parameters, licenses, release dates, language support and latencies reflect supplied source claims. Key Considerations for Selection: • Licensing: For commercial use, prioritize Apache 2.0 or MIT licensed models like Kokoro, Chatterbox, Qwen3-TTS, and CosyVoice2. Avoid CC BY-NC or Research licenses (e.g., Fish Audio, Breeze TTS 2) for products. • Hardware: Kokoro and Piper run on CPU. Chatterbox and CosyVoice2 fit on consumer GPUs (4–6GB VRAM). Voxtral and Fish Audio require 12–16GB+ VRAM. • Language: Fish Audio and OmniVoice support 80–600+ languages. Kokoro and Dia are English-only. Chatterbox supports 23+ languages. • Voice Cloning: XTTS v2, Fish Audio, and Chatterbox support zero-shot cloning. Kokoro and Piper do not. • Latency: Voxtral (~90ms) and CosyVoice2 (~150ms) are optimized for real-time streaming. Bark and Tortoise are slower.
